THE STUDIO

Small studio.
Personal attention.

A neighbourhood place for a haircut, a conversation and a little time for yourself.

Start with a conversation.

LINE is a student concept for a local barber studio in Astana. Our approach is simple: understand the person in the chair, take time with the cut and make every visit feel comfortable.

From a clean taper to a familiar classic, we focus on shape, texture and a finish you can manage at home.

What to expect

  • A short consultation before every cut
  • Clean tools and a tidy workspace
  • Advice on everyday styling
  • Clear prices before we begin
